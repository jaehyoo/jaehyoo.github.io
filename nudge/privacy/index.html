Nudge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Nudge ("the app") is an Android app that works as a Bluetooth touchpad and mouse jiggler, or jiggles a physical mouse placed on the phone screen. It is developed by jaehyoo ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Summary

  • We do not run any servers and do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • Settings and the last connected PC stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows an ad from Google AdMob when you start the jiggler, at most once every 10 minutes, and AdMob collects some device information as described below.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your settings, such as interval, movement size, language and auto-stop options.
  • The Bluetooth address of the PC you last connected to, so the app can reconnect to it.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information.

Bluetooth

The app registers as a Bluetooth mouse and sends mouse movement and click data only to the PC you choose to connect. It reads the names and addresses of devices already paired with your phone, and of nearby devices when you search for a PC, only to show them in the app. This information is not stored beyond the last connected PC and is not shared.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to pair with, find and connect to your PC.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only): Android requires it to search for Bluetooth devices on these versions. The app does not read or use your location.
  • Notifications: to show that the jiggler is running.
  • Wake lock: to keep the jiggler running in the background and with the screen off. The app does not ask for a battery optimization exemption itself; it only opens your phone's battery settings so you can turn optimization off for Nudge if you choose.
  • Vibration: used only if you turn on vibration in "Mouse on screen" mode.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob when you press start, at most once every 10 minutes. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ads > Ad privacy settings in the app. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

After you buy "Remove ads", the app no longer loads ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
