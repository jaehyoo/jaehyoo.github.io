Tug o' Sea Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Tug o' Sea ("the game", released in Korean as Tug o' Sea: 물 만난 다이버) is an Android arcade game about diving and spearfishing at sites modelled on famous dives around the world. It is developed by jaehyoo ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

Summary

  • We do not run any servers and do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • Your settings stay on your phone. If you choose cloud save and sign in to Google Play Games, your progress is also kept in your Play Games saved games, so it follows you to another phone.
  • The free version shows an ad from Google AdMob when a dive starts, at most once every 10 minutes, and AdMob collects some device information as described below.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • The game has no accounts of its own and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game stores the following only on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your progress: total score, best score for each dive site, and which sites you have cleared and how many times.
  • Your settings: whether music, sound effects and vibration are on.

This information does not identify you. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

Google Play Games

Cloud save is optional. The game offers it once, after you clear your first dive site, and you can also turn it on later with "Cloud save" under Settings on the title screen. If you sign in to Google Play Games there, the game signs in again each time it starts, and keeps a copy of the progress listed above (no settings) in your Play Games saved games, stored by Google in your Google account, and adds progress made on your other phones to the copy on this one.

While you are signed in, the game also reads your Play Games gamer name, to show it on the title screen and in the hall of fame, and sends your total score to the game's Play Games leaderboard. That leaderboard is public: other players can see your gamer name and score on it, in the hall of fame and in Google Play Games. You can change your gamer name, or who can see your Play Games activity, in your Play Games profile settings. The game does not read your email or friends list.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it. You can delete it, and the game's other Play Games data, in the Google Play Games app or at your Google Account.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the progress and settings above in your Google account backup, so they can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that backup under the Google Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for in-game effects. You can turn vibration off under Settings on the title screen or in the pause menu.
  • Internet and network state: added by Google's ads, billing and Play Games libraries, to load ads, to check purchases with Google Play and, if you choose cloud save, to reach Play Games.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described below.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob when a dive starts, at most once every 10 minutes. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ad privacy settings on the title screen. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

After you buy "Remove ads", the game no longer loads ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback" in the game, your email app opens a message to us with the game version, Android version and phone model filled in. You can edit or delete these before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change would mean collecting personal information, the game will tell you before it takes effect.
