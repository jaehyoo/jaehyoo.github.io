Tug o' Sea Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Tug o' Sea ("the game", released in Korean as Tug o' Sea: 물 만난 다이버) is an arcade game about diving and spearfishing at sites modelled on famous dives around the world. It is developed by jaehyoo ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

The game comes in three versions, and this one policy covers them all:

  • Android phones and tablets, from Google Play.
  • Android TV, from Google Play. It shows no ads and sells nothing in the game.
  • PC, from Steam. It shows no ads, sells nothing in the game and uses no Google services.

Where a section applies only to some versions, it says which.

Summary

  • We do not run any servers and do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. On Android, if you choose cloud save and sign in to Google Play Games, your progress is also kept in your Play Games saved games, so it follows you to another device.
  • On phones and tablets, the free version shows an ad from Google AdMob when a dive starts, at most once every 10 minutes. You can also choose to watch a rewarded ad for pearls or a tank pack. AdMob collects some device information as described below. The Android TV and PC versions show no ads.
  • On phones and tablets, the "Remove ads" purchase and the pearl bundles are handled entirely by Google Play. The PC version is bought on Steam, which handles that purchase.
  • The game has no accounts of its own and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Your progress: total score, best score for each dive site, which sites you have cleared and how many times, and which seasonal event sites you have cleared in which year.
  • Your pearls and gear: the counts the pearl balance is worked out from (sites cleared, minerals and litter found and sold, rewarded ads watched, the days you were paid for your first dive and for sharing the game, pearl dives completed, gear bought and used up), and, on phones and tablets, for each pearl bundle you buy, the Google Play order number and the pearls it gave, so an order is never counted twice.
  • Your settings: whether music, sound effects and vibration are on, and on PC, the language you picked.

This information does not identify you. On Android, uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it. On PC it is kept in the game's folder in your user profile (on Windows, %APPDATA%\TugOSea), and deleting that folder removes it; uninstalling the game through Steam may leave it in place.

Sharing (phones and tablets)

When you choose to share the game, it hands a short introduction and its Google Play link to the app you pick from Android's share sheet. When you share a site or sea you have cleared, it sends a picture of the clear screen with the same introduction and link drawn along its bottom, through one temporary copy in the game's own storage that the next share replaces. What happens to it after that is up to the app you picked. The game does not know whom you share with or whether the share was sent; the pearls for sharing the game are paid once a day when you come back from the share sheet.

Google Play Games (Android phones, tablets and TV)

Cloud save is optional. The game offers it once, after you clear your first dive site, and you can also turn it on later with "Cloud save" under Settings on the title screen. If you sign in to Google Play Games there, the game signs in again each time it starts, and keeps a copy of the progress, pearls and gear listed above (no settings) in your Play Games saved games, stored by Google in your Google account, and adds progress made on your other devices to the copy on this one.

While you are signed in, the game also reads your Play Games gamer name, to show it on the title screen and in the hall of fame, and sends your total score, and how many dive sites and levels you have cleared, to the game's Play Games leaderboards. Those leaderboards are public: other players can see your gamer name and these numbers on them, in the hall of fame and in Google Play Games. You can change your gamer name, or who can see your Play Games activity, in your Play Games profile settings. The game does not read your email or friends list.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it. You can delete it, and the game's other Play Games data, in the Google Play Games app or at your Google Account.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the progress and settings above in your Google account backup, so they can be restored on a new device. Google handles that backup under the Google Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

PC version (Steam)

The PC version has no ads, no in-game purchases and no Google services, and it does not connect to the internet itself. Steam handles buying, downloading and updating the game, and whatever Steam collects in doing so is covered by the Steam Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Steam account details or payment details. The game does not currently use Steam achievements, leaderboards or Steam Cloud; if an update adds any of them, this policy will be updated first.

If a game controller is connected, the game reads its buttons and sticks to play, and sends vibration to it if vibration is on. Nothing about the controller is stored or sent anywhere.

Android permissions

  • Vibration: for in-game effects. You can turn vibration off under Settings on the title screen or in the pause menu.
  • Internet and network state: added by Google's ads, billing and Play Games libraries, to load ads, to check purchases with Google Play and, if you choose cloud save, to reach Play Games.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described below.

Advertising (phones and tablets)

The free version shows a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob when a dive starts, at most once every 10 minutes. The game also offers rewarded ads from Google AdMob that you choose to watch: one in the shop for pearls, and one for a tank pack when you have neither pearls nor packs left. They play only when you tap them. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ad privacy settings on the title screen. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

After you buy "Remove ads", the game no longer shows the ad when a dive starts. The rewarded ad in the shop stays available, so the game still loads one in case you choose to watch it.

Purchases

On phones and tablets, the one-time "Remove ads" purchase and the pearl bundles are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, with its order number, so it can turn off the start ad, add the pearls you bought, and restore "Remove ads" on your other devices. The order numbers of pearl bundles are kept with your progress as described above; they are not sent to us. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

The Android TV version sells nothing, but it checks with Google Play whether "Remove ads" was bought with the same Google account, so the purchase applies there too. The PC version is bought on Steam and has no purchases inside the game.

Feedback (Android)

If you choose "Send feedback" in the game, your email app opens a message to us with the game version, Android version and device model filled in. You can edit or delete these before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change would mean collecting personal information, the game will tell you before it takes effect.
