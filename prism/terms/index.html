Using the app

The app shows the frequency spectrum of the sound picked up by your phone's microphone, or of the sound other apps play on your phone. You may use it for personal and work purposes, subject to these terms and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Measurements

Levels are shown in dBFS, relative to the full scale of the phone's audio input. Phone microphones are not calibrated and their response varies between models, so the readings are not sound pressure levels and must not be relied on for hearing protection, workplace noise assessment, legal or safety purposes, or any other use that needs a calibrated instrument.
