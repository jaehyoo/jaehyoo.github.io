Prism Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Prism ("the app") is an Android app that shows the frequency spectrum of the sound picked up by the phone's microphone, or of the sound other apps play on the phone. It is developed by jaehyoo ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Sound is analyzed on your phone as it arrives and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • We do not run any servers and do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows an ad from Google AdMob when you open the settings, at most once every 10 minutes, and AdMob collects some device information as described below.
  • The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Audio

While the analyzer screen is open, the app reads short blocks of audio, turns them into frequency levels and draws them, then discards the audio. Nothing is written to storage and no audio leaves the phone. The app's internet access is used only by the Google AdMob and Google Play Billing libraries described below, which never receive audio.

When you choose internal audio as the input, Android asks you to allow screen capture. The app uses this only to receive the sound other apps play (media, games and unlabeled audio, as Android allows); it does not capture or read the screen image. Calls, notifications and system sounds are not captured, and apps can block capture of their own sound. A notification is shown for as long as the capture is active, and you can stop it from there or by switching back to the microphone.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Your settings, such as the band resolution, peak hold, theme, language, input and screen rotation.
  • Whether you bought "Remove ads", and when the last ad was shown, so ads stay at most once every 10 minutes.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to analyze the sound around you. Android also requires it for capturing internal audio.
  • Screen capture (internal audio only): Android grants access to other apps' sound through its screen capture consent; you are asked each time you start it.
  • Notifications: asked before the first internal audio capture, to show that the capture is active and let you stop it.
  • Internet and network state: used by Google AdMob to load ads and by Google Play Billing for the purchase.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described under Advertising.
  • Foreground service: to keep the internal audio capture open while you switch between the analyzer and its settings, with a notification showing it is active.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob when you open the settings, at most once every 10 minutes. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ads > Ad privacy settings in the app. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

After you buy "Remove ads", the app no longer loads ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase, which also unlocks the color themes and the light/dark choice, is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on your other devices. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
